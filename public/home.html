Why it's safe

Your ledger is yours. We just read it — and only once you say so.

Financial data does not belong on a public page. That's why there is nothing behind this site to look at until you authenticate: no preview dashboard, no sample tenant, no numbers of any kind. Sign in and your organisation's data loads for your session only.

  • Xero OAuth 2.0 — we never receive your Xero credentials.
  • Read-only access to the organisation you authorise. Nothing is written back to your books.
  • Every dashboard and API route requires a live session — no data is served to anonymous visitors.
  • Disconnect at any time from your Xero account settings and access ends immediately.
